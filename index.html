I · About the Application

Musically Structured Reading Exercises

At First Sight is an application built for pianists who need consistent, fresh sight-reading material without exhausting repertoire libraries or memorizing predictable note patterns.

Rather than relying on static musical fragments, the application employs a procedural music engine that builds balanced two-handed exercises from fundamental voice-leading, harmony, rhythm, and accompaniment structures. Every piece is engraved directly to standard music notation, rendered faithfully across iOS, iPadOS, and accompanied by quick flashcard recognition on watchOS.

III · Frequent Inquiries

Questions & Guidance

How do I restore an existing subscription or purchase?

If you have reinstalled the app or set up a new Apple device, open At First Sight, navigate to the Settings screen (or the Subscription page), and select Restore Purchases. This will query your Apple ID and restore your active entitlement immediately without charging your account.

How do I cancel or modify my subscription?

All subscriptions are processed securely by Apple through your Apple ID. To manage or cancel renewals at any time:

Open the Settings app on your iOS device → tap your Name / Apple ID banner at the top → select Subscriptions → tap At First Sight to view expiration dates or turn off auto-renewal.

How does procedural exercise generation work?

Rather than stitching together pre-recorded MIDI files or random pitch sequences, the generator implements classical harmonic progressions (tonic, subdominant, dominant preparations, cadences) alongside balanced melodic contours and left-hand accompaniment textures (broken chords, Alberti bass, choral voicing). This ensures exercises read naturally like authentic piano literature.

What features are included in the Apple Watch companion app?

The watchOS application is designed for quick, focused note-identification drills on the go. It renders clean single-staff and grand-staff noteheads directly on your wrist, allowing you to sharpen clef reading speed during brief moments away from the keyboard.

Can I submit feature suggestions or pedagogical requests?

Yes. We welcome feedback from piano instructors, conservatory students, and practicing musicians. Please email your thoughts directly to colbycabrera.wd@gmail.com.
